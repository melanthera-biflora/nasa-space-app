- [x] **Smoke days: count only medium and heavy smoke (2011 on)?** Yes (D37). Light smoke drives most of the rise. From 2022, light-smoke days jump everywhere and in every season (winter about ten times its old level) while medium-and-heavy days follow the real fire years (2022 is quiet). Over 2011–25, 93% of lower-48 squares show a clear rise with any density against 41% for medium and heavy, and Phoenix and Minneapolis lose their clear rise. HMS changed its density labels in 2022, and Liu et al. 2024 (Int. J. Wildland Fire 33, WF23148) find light plumes cause "large overestimates in HMS-derived smoke days and trends" and recommend heavy-only or medium-and-heavy days. Until decided, the animated map shows a note when smoke days are on screen.
